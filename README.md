# SousAgent

A home sous-chef for AI agents: a set of skills that know your kitchen, your pantry,
your allergies and your tastes — and help you decide what to cook and how long it
will *really* take.

Built for people who enjoy cooking — the process, the result, or both.

## What it does

- Keeps a living profile: equipment (with the details that matter — size, material,
  lid, oven-safe), pantry, people you cook for, kitchens you cook in, and where you are.
- Treats allergies and intolerances as hard rules: checks hidden sources, flags
  risky dishes, offers substitutions.
- Suggests dishes for different situations: a quick weeknight meal, exploring
  something new, cooking for a crowd, using up what's in the fridge, and more.
- Gives honest time estimates: total time from start to finish (overnight soaking
  included) and how much of it you actually spend chopping, stirring and watching.
- Updates and saves your profile as you talk ("I just bought a stand mixer").

## What it doesn't do

- Meal planning, shopping lists or nutrition tracking.
- Replace medical advice. It helps you avoid allergens, but always check labels.
- Lock you into one AI tool. Skills follow the open `SKILL.md` format and plain
  Markdown files.

## How to use it

Set up your own private copy (see [SETUP.md](SETUP.md)), open it with your AI agent,
and say hi — it will walk you through a short onboarding. After that, just talk:
*"Something quick for two, I have leftover rice"* or *"I want to try something new
this weekend."*
