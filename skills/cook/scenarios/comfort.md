# Scenario: comfort

The user wants food that feels familiar and comforting. Comfort is personal: it
comes from upbringing, memories and habits, not from a generic list.

## In the overview

- Why it's comforting (familiar dish, flavor, the process itself).
- Rough effort.

## Before suggesting

- **Comfort dishes:** start from the person's comfort dishes and comforting
  flavors in their profile.
- **Mood or situation:** sick, cold, tired, hungover, homesick — each calls for
  something different. Ask only if it isn't obvious.
- **Effort:** comfort can be in the result (something quick and familiar) or in
  the process (kneading dough, stirring a risotto for half an hour). Infer from the
  request ("I'm exhausted" vs. "slow Sunday"), ask if unclear. Combines with
  `quick`.

## Choosing

- Familiar, not new. No surprises, no stretching.
- No "healthier versions" unless asked.
- If a dish from the person's background needs ingredients that are hard to get
  in this region, say what's missing and find the closest faithful version.

## Afterwards

If a dish clearly hit the spot and isn't in the person's comfort dishes yet, ask
whether to add it.
